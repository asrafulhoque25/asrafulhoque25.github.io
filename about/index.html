Who is Md Asraful Hoque?

I'm Md Asraful Hoque, and most people who know me call me Emon. I'm a full-stack web developer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I build complete websites, web apps and online stores: the interface people see, the CMS or Shopify store a team runs every day, the AI features that save them time, and the backend and integrations that keep it running.

I started on the frontend in 2018 and never lost the eye for detail that comes with it. Today I take projects from a Figma file to a deployed site, working in React and Next.js for products and dashboards, Shopify for e-commerce, and custom WordPress themes and Webflow for marketing and agency sites. More and more of my work now adds AI on top: assistants, smarter search and automation.

What I work on

  • React & Next.js development: SEO-ready web apps, SaaS marketing sites and dashboards with server rendering and clean component systems.
  • Shopify store development: store setup, custom themes and edits in Liquid, Figma to Shopify, apps, speed and SEO.
  • AI integration: AI chatbots trained on a business's own content, AI search and product recommendations, and workflow automation with OpenAI, Claude and Gemini APIs.
  • WordPress development: custom themes coded from a design, custom post types and fields, Elementor builds and fixes, plus speed and SEO setup.
  • Webflow development: pixel-perfect builds with CMS collections and interactions that a marketing team can edit on its own.
  • Backend, CMS & integrations: REST APIs, headless CMS setups, forms, CRM hooks, deployment and hosting.

Recent launches include the websites for Pixxen, Pexnet, Quecut and Wadi Al Bada IT Solutions in Dubai. You can see more in my project index.

My journey

  • 2022 – present: Full-Stack Web Developer on agency and client projects, now including Shopify stores and AI integrations.
  • 2019 – 2022: Senior Frontend Developer at Getweb, building pixel-perfect frontends and Webflow sites for international clients.
  • 2018 – 2019: Junior Frontend Developer (intern) at Creative IT Institute.
  • 2016 – 2018: SEO Executive in sales operations, where I learned how websites actually get found.

How I like to work

One developer, one clear process: brief, design, build, launch. Clients get a staging link while I build and a site they can manage after launch. Because of my early years in SEO, every build starts with semantic markup, compressed images, clean meta tags and schema, so it is ready for Google and AI search from day one. I write about this on my blog.
